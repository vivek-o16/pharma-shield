How it works

Three steps, run entirely in your browser.

  1. 01

    Enter drug details

    Type the medicine name and, if you have it, the batch number printed on the pack.

  2. 02

    Database check

    Pharma Shield compares what you entered against the available CDSCO dataset, ignoring case and extra spaces.

  3. 03

    Get your result

    You receive a clear red, yellow or green result, along with related medicine information.

About this project

Pharma Shield is an educational drug-quality awareness platform designed to help users identify medicines and batches reported in available CDSCO quality alerts.

It was built as a college Innovation Practical project to demonstrate how a simple, transparent lookup tool can make publicly available drug-alert information easier to search. The dataset shipped with this demo is illustrative sample data, clearly marked as such — it is not an official CDSCO feed.

CDSCO source

Official quality alerts and NSQ notices are published by the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO), Government of India.

Visit cdsco.gov.in

Disclaimer

Pharma Shield is an educational project that checks medicine names and batch numbers against the CDSCO quality alert data available in its database.

"No alert found" does not guarantee that a medicine is safe, genuine or free from quality issues. It only means that no matching record was found in the available dataset.

Medicine information is provided for educational purposes only. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.
